Even – Privacy Policy

Applies to the Even iPhone app and the Even website. Last updated: 18 November 2026.

Even helps you keep track of your balance: money in, money out, and what is left. This page explains what information the app keeps and what happens to it, in plain words.

What Even stores

  • Your account: your email address and a password (or your Google sign-in on the website), so you can sign in.
  • What you add: amounts, whether they are money in or out, categories, the notes you write, dates, your starting balance and your recurring items.

That's all. Even does not ask for your name, location, contacts, photos or bank details.

Why

Only to make the app work: to show you your balance and statistics, and to keep your data the same on your iPhone and on the website.

Where it is kept

Your account and data are stored with Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), which runs the app's sign-in and database. Security rules make sure each person can only read and change their own data. Information is sent over encrypted connections.

What Even does not do

  • No ads.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites, and no analytics.
  • Your data is never sold or shared with anyone, other than Google Firebase storing it for the app as described above.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account and all your data at any time in the iPhone app: Account → Delete Account. This removes your entries, settings and sign-in account permanently, on every device. Single entries can be deleted by swiping them to the left.

Children

Even is a general money app and is not aimed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
